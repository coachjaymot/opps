What does OPPS mean?

TURN OPPS INTO OPPORTUNITIES.

In everyday language, an “opp” can mean an opponent, rival, or enemy. We took that familiar word and gave it a different purpose.

At OPPS Basketball, the name stands for opportunities—to be seen, to belong, to develop, to compete, and to build a future through sport.

We believe athletes are more than rankings, reputations, or the circumstances they come from. Talent deserves development. Effort deserves a chance. And competition should challenge players without closing doors on their potential.
